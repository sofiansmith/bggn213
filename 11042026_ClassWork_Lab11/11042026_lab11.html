Background

We ran Alphafold2 from colabfold.

We will

  1. Read all model AF2 files

  2. Calculate RMSD between all models

  3. Calculate RMSF between all residues of all models

  4. Examine AF2 scores pLDDT and PAE

  5. Calculate residue conservation scores for all residues
